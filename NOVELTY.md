# Project Novelty

POLARIS is not simply an inventory tracker, cargo dashboard, or a chatbot. Its novelty lies in the integration of dependency-aware expedition intelligence.

The system connects:
- Transport disruption
- Cargo impact
- Inventory depletion
- Asset degradation
- Mission impact

It combines these domains with:
- Forecasting
- Shortage prediction
- Optimization
- Explainable risk intelligence
- Human decision support

This makes it more than a monitoring tool. It is a coordinated risk-explanation platform that helps expedition managers understand why a delay matters and which intervention is most appropriate.

The core idea is that a disruption in one domain can cascade across the full expedition network. POLARIS makes that path explicit and actionable.
